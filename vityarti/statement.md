# Project Statement: CampusBite (Smart Canteen Management System)

## 1. Problem Statement
Traditional university and institutional canteens face chronic operational challenges during peak rush intervals (breakfast breaks, post-lecture lunch hours, and evening intervals). Students and faculty members routinely endure long physical queues at billing counters followed by congested crowds at food pickup stations. Manual cash handling, paper tokens, and lack of live order status visibility result in order mix-ups, food wastage, delayed class attendance, and an inability for canteen administrators to accurately forecast daily inventory requirements.

**CampusBite** resolves these issues by digitizing the end-to-end campus dining lifecycle into a unified, high-speed, and accessible web-based Canteen Management System.

---

## 2. Scope of the Project
The scope of **CampusBite** encompasses the core dining operations of a university campus:
- **Digital Ordering & Browsing**: Full access to categorized menus (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Beverages, Healthy) with real-time stock levels, preparation times, dietary tags, and caloric metrics.
- **Integrated Digital Student Wallet**: Cashless transactions with instant mock balance recharges, automatic deductions, and real-time transaction ledger.
- **Token-Based Queuing & Pickups**: Generation of unique sequential digital tokens (e.g. `TK-105`) accompanied by printable thermal-style slips with QR verification codes.
- **Kitchen Production Management (KDS POS)**: Live interactive order board for canteen staff with 3 operational stages: *Pending*, *In Preparation*, and *Ready for Pickup*, with instant audio/visual notifications.
- **Canteen Administration & Operational Analytics**: Comprehensive control center for inventory adjustments, real-time revenue KPIs, top-selling product metrics, category breakdowns, and audit CSV exports.

---

## 3. Target Users
1. **Students**: Primary consumers seeking fast menu discovery, dietary filtering, cashless wallet payments, and minimal physical waiting time.
2. **Faculty & Staff Members**: Campus professionals requiring rapid order turnaround during scheduled lecture breaks.
3. **Canteen Chefs & Kitchen Staff**: Kitchen personnel who rely on the live Kitchen Display System (KDS) to prioritize incoming order tickets, monitor preparation times, and notify customers when trays are ready.
4. **Canteen Managers & Administrators**: Operations managers overseeing menu pricing, stock availability, daily revenue collection, and sales auditing.

---

## 4. High-Level Features
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Secure access tailored to Students, Faculty, Kitchen Staff, and System Administrators, accompanied by a 1-click live persona switcher for evaluations.
- **Dynamic Menu Catalog**: Instant keystroke search and multi-criteria filters for categories and dietary preferences (*Pure Veg*, *Vegan*, *Non-Veg*).
- **Interactive Cart & Special Instructions Drawer**: Floating meal tray supporting quantity adjustments, custom chef notes (e.g. "less spicy"), and live subtotal calculations.
- **Digital Token & Receipt Engine**: Automated creation of sequential tokens with printable thermal-ticket formatting and simulated counter QR codes.
- **Live Kitchen Kanban Board**: 3-swimlane visual workflow for cooking staff with automated real-time polling every 4 seconds.
- **Executive Analytics Dashboard**: Gross sales KPI scorecards, category distribution bar charts, top-ranking dishes, and downloadable CSV financial reports.
